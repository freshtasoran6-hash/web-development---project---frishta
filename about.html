The Biography of Our Sweets

The Story of Flan

Flan dates back to ancient Rome! It was the Romans who first domesticated chickens and found themselves with a surplus of eggs, leading to the creation of this beautiful, sweet custard topped with caramel.


The Pancake's Journey

Pancakes have been a breakfast staple for centuries! The ancient Greeks and Romans ate a version made from wheat flour, olive oil, honey, and curdled milk. Today, we load them with everything from Nutella to fresh berries!
